    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Last updated: 21 November 2026 (B.E. 2569)

    Answers to what visitors most often ask about this site, visiting Lopburi’s old town, the 3D models and the guestbook. Where the site cannot verify something, we say so rather than guess.

    About this website

    Is this the official website of Lopburi province?

    No. This site was compiled by the project owner for education and tourism. Information may be inaccurate or change, so always check opening times and visiting conditions with the body that looks after each place before you travel.

    Which places are on this site?

    There are currently 12 places in Lopburi province, from Dvaravati traces (Wat Nakhon Kosa) and Khmer art (Prang Khaek, Phra Prang Sam Yot, San Phra Kan, Wat Phra Si Rattana Mahathat) to the Ayutthaya and King Narai period (King Narai’s Palace, Wat Lai, Wat Sao Thong Thong, Baan Wichayen, Wat Kawisararam Ratchaworawihan) Khao Wong Phra Chan, and Ang Sap Lek, a reservoir with a local tale tied to King Narai’s time. You can filter by era or category on the home page.

    Where does the information come from, and how reliable is it?

    Each place page lists its sources at the bottom, and the “Credits & policy” menu gathers all of them. The text is compiled from published articles, travel sites and research; it is not official government information.

    When sources disagree or cannot be confirmed, the site says “according to tradition” or “it is reported that”, and does not add details that cannot be checked. If you find a mistake, please tell us.

    Report a mistake or suggest a fix
    How do I change the language?

    Press the language button at the top right and choose Thai, English, Chinese or Japanese. Each language has its own address and the site remembers your choice. The Chinese and Japanese translations are still drafts awaiting a native-speaker check, and text that visitors write themselves is not translated.

    Getting around and visiting

    What are the opening hours and entrance fees?

    The site does not list opening hours or fees yet, because there is no verifiable official source and these details change. Check with the Fine Arts Department, local authorities or the caretaker of each place before you go. Temples and shrines are still active places of worship, so their hours can differ from other historic sites.

    How do I get to Lopburi?

    The site has no verified travel information for getting to Lopburi, so it gives no timetables or fares. Use the “Navigate with Google Maps” button on each place page to plan a route from your location. The button searches by place name, so check the position on the map before you set out.

    How long does it take to see everything?

    The old-town walking route on the home page is planned to fit in one day, starting at King Narai’s Palace and ending at Wat Nakhon Kosa, following historic route A on the map. Wat Lai, Khao Wong Phra Chan and Ang Sap Lek are not part of this route, so plan separate time for them.

    What should I wear?

    Dress modestly, especially at temples, San Phra Kan and places still used for ceremonies: for example, a top with sleeves, trousers or a skirt that covers the knees, and follow signs or staff instructions, such as taking off shoes before entering a building.

    How should I deal with the monkeys?

    The monkeys in Lopburi live around San Phra Kan and Phra Prang Sam Yot. The credits menu lists articles and news about them if you want to read more.

    General advice: keep valuables, glasses, phones and food bags out of sight, do not carry food openly, do not tease or stare at them, do not reach toward them, and follow local signs and staff advice.

    3D models and AR

    How do I view the 3D models?

    Press the cover image of a place (on the home page or the place page) to load its model, then drag to rotate, double-click to reset, or use the arrow keys. Model files only load when you press, which keeps pages quick to open.

    Which devices support the “View in AR” button?

    Open the site over https:// on a phone or tablet that supports AR: Android with Chrome and Google Play Services for AR, or an iPhone/iPad with Safari. If your device does not support AR you can still view the model full screen and rotate it.

    How closely do the models match the real places?

    The models were made by the project owner (credited under each model) as a learning aid. They are not surveys or archaeological reconstructions, so details and proportions may differ from the real places.

    Guestbook, reviews and privacy

    Who can see what I write in the guestbook?

    Check the status line under the form. If it says “Shared with everyone (server)”, your message is shown to all visitors. If it says “Offline mode”, it is kept only in your own browser. Do not include links or personal details such as phone numbers or email addresses. Moderators may hide or delete inappropriate messages.

    How are place reviews different from the guestbook?

    Only where the review feature is enabled, place pages have a “Visitor reviews” section at the bottom. You must give 1–5 stars, and a review appears after a moderator approves it, while guestbook entries on the home page appear straight away. There is spam protection, such as an automated-submission check and rate limits, so you may see a “sending too fast” message if you send repeatedly.

    How is my data stored, and can I ask for it to be deleted?

    The privacy policy explains what is stored, what it is used for, and how to contact us to ask for deletion.
