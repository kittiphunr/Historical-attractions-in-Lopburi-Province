    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 21 November 2026 (B.E. 2569)

    This website compiles information on the historic sites of Lopburi Province for education and tourism. It is not the province's official website. We collect as little personal data as possible: there is no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.

    01Who runs this site

    This site is run by the project owner (Kittiphun Ruttanakorn), who is the data controller under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA) for the data collected through this site's guestbook.

    02What we collect and why

    Guestbook and reviews: when you write a message or a review we store the name you enter, your message, the star rating and place you choose (if any) and the time it was sent, in order to show it in the guestbook or on the place page to all visitors (reviews are shown after a moderator checks them). This information is public, so please do not include personal details such as a phone number or email.

    Spam protection: when the shared guestbook is enabled, the system stores a hash of your IP address (one-way SHA-256 with a secret salt, truncated) alongside the message, only to limit how many messages one person can send. We do not store your actual IP address, and the hash cannot be reversed into one. Before sending, your browser automatically solves a small verification puzzle (a proof-of-work CAPTCHA) that stores nothing extra and contacts no outside service, unless the site owner chooses Cloudflare Turnstile, which sends browser signals and your IP address to Cloudflare to confirm you are human. If the site owner turns on notifications, a new review or message (the display name and the text) is emailed to the site owner through the host's mail system so it can be checked before approval.

    Data in your browser: the site keeps your choices in your own browser (localStorage/sessionStorage): day/night mode, language, a page-transition flag, and your guestbook messages when the shared guestbook is not enabled. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser. The site sets no cookies of its own.

    03Third-party services

    When you open this site, your browser connects to the external services below, which may see your IP address and browser details under their own policies. We do not control what they collect.

    • GitHub Pages — hosts the website (GitHub, Inc.)
    • Google Fonts — the Prompt typeface (Google LLC)
    • Open-Meteo.com — weather data. Your browser sends only the coordinates of Lopburi town, not your own location.
    • Guestbook and review database — stores and serves the messages, running on the website host's MySQL database or on Cloudflare Workers/D1 (Cloudflare, Inc.), only when the site owner has enabled it
    • YouTube and Google Maps — only links that open in a new tab when you click them. If videos are embedded in future, the youtube-nocookie.com domain is used.

    043D models and AR

    3D models are rendered in your browser and no personal data is sent out. When you tap “View in AR”, your device opens its own AR viewer (such as WebXR, Google Scene Viewer or Apple Quick Look), which asks to use your device's camera. Camera images are not sent to this website.

    05Retention and deletion

    Guestbook messages and reviews on the server are kept until the site owner deletes them (on request or because they are inappropriate); reviews that fail moderation may be deleted. The Cloudflare system keeps only the latest 2,000 messages and deletes older ones automatically. Messages stored in your browser stay until you clear the site's data.

    06Your rights

    Under the PDPA you may request access to and a copy of your data, ask for corrections, ask for deletion or destruction, ask us to restrict its use, object to its collection, use or disclosure, and withdraw consent at any time, as well as complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee. To have your message removed from the guestbook, tell us the name you used and the content of the message through the channel below, since we hold no other identifying information beyond what you entered.

    07Children

    This website is not aimed at collecting data from children. If a parent finds that a child has written personal details in the guestbook, please tell us so we can remove the message.

    08Changes to this policy

    If how we collect or use data changes, we will update this page and the date above.

    Contact

    For questions about personal data or to have a message removed, use the project's Issues page on GitHub (it is public, so please do not post personal details — give only the name used and the content of the message to remove).

      Credits and sources

      Photo and video policy: This site does not embed other people's photos or videos without stating the source. Each real photo shows the photographer, licence and source link under the image, and videos show the channel name and a link to YouTube, the rights holder.

      3D models: The models of all 11 sites and the whole-province map model (.glb files) were made by the project owner, rendered with Three.js (MIT), with a CSS 3D fallback; they are not adapted from other people's photos or models.

      Lopburi provincial seal: An image provided by the project owner, used to illustrate the content and identity of this site. Rights in the seal belong to the issuing authority. This is not the official website of Lopburi Province.

      Home-page cover image: A panoramic composite (Phra Prang Sam Yot, a railway beside the water, a white building with a red roof) provided by the project owner. It is assembled from several parts whose photographers or creators and licences are not yet known, so it is credited only as provided by the project owner. If the sources become known, credits will be added here.

      AR: Uses <model-viewer> by Google (Apache-2.0), supporting WebXR on Android Chrome, Scene Viewer, and Quick Look on iPhone/iPad. Requires HTTPS and an AR-capable device.

      Weather: Data from Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), fetched in the visitor's browser, sending only the coordinates of Lopburi town.

      Please note: Opening hours, fees and transport can change. Please check with the Fine Arts Department or the 4th Regional Office of Fine Arts, Lopburi before travelling.
